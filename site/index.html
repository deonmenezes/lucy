01 / What this actually is

Most AI forgets you
the second you hang up.

Lucy does the opposite. Memory is not a feature bolted onto her. It is the whole point.

📞

It is a phone call, not a chat window

No app to install, no account to make, no browser tab. You dial a number the same way you would call a person. Landline, flip phone, anything.

🧠

She keeps a real memory of you

Every word of every call is written to disk as it happens. When the call ends she distills it into durable facts about your life and files them under your number.

🔎

She can search her own history

Ask "what did I tell you about my sister?" and she runs a full-text search across every conversation you have ever had, then answers from what she finds.

🏠

Her memory lives on one machine

A single SQLite file. No cloud vector database, no third-party memory service, no analytics pipeline. You can open it, read it, or delete it.

🌙

She answers around the clock

She runs as a background service that starts on boot and restarts itself if it falls over. 3am counts.

🍈

Built on Guava AI

The voice, the telephony, the turn-taking and the language model all come from the Guava voice agent platform. The memory is the part built on top.

04 / Under the hood

How she remembers

A conversation is not saved as one blob at the end. It is captured continuously, then distilled. This is the actual lifecycle in lucy.py, and the numbering is the real order of operations on every call.

  1. The phone rings and she looks you up

    Your caller ID is the key. on_call_start loads your profile, known facts and past summaries, then hands them to her with add_info before she says a single word.

  2. Every sentence hits the disk immediately

    on_caller_speech and on_agent_speech append each turn as it is spoken. If the power cut mid-sentence, everything up to that word is already saved.

  3. Questions trigger a search of her history

    on_question runs an FTS5 ranked search over every turn, fact and summary filed under your number, then answers using only what came back.

  4. When you hang up, she works out what mattered

    on_session_end reads the whole transcript and extracts your name, a summary, and the durable facts worth keeping. Small talk is dropped. This is the step that makes call eleven feel like a friendship.

~/.lucy/memory.db · the entire memory
-- one row per person, keyed on their number
CREATE TABLE callers (
  phone      TEXT PRIMARY KEY,
  name       TEXT,
  call_count INTEGER
);

-- every utterance, written live
CREATE TABLE turns (
  call_id  TEXT,
  speaker  TEXT,
  text     TEXT,
  ts       TEXT
);

-- what she chose to keep
CREATE TABLE facts (
  phone      TEXT,
  fact       TEXT,
  created_at TEXT
);

-- how she finds any of it again
CREATE VIRTUAL TABLE search
  USING fts5(phone, kind, ref, text);
05 / The engine

Powered by 🍈 Guava AI

Lucy is not a phone system. She is roughly three hundred lines of Python sitting on top of Guava, the voice agent platform that does the genuinely hard parts: carrying a real phone number, streaming audio both ways, knowing when you have finished a sentence, and sounding like a person while doing it.

Guava handles

Telephony, the live number, speech in and out, turn-taking, interruptions, the language model, and the voice itself.

Lucy adds

Identity per caller, a durable local memory, end-of-call distillation, and full-text recall across every conversation.

Why it matters

Memory is the layer Guava deliberately leaves to you, which is exactly why a friend is buildable in a single file.

06 / Straight answers

What she cannot do yet

Worth saying plainly, because a friend that oversells itself is not much of a friend.

🙅 She talks, she does not act

She has no calendar, inbox, or web access. She listens, remembers and helps you think. She cannot yet go and do things on your behalf.

📵 Memory is tied to your number

Call from a withheld or different number and she will not know you. Callers who hide their number share one anonymous profile.

💤 A sleeping machine answers nothing

She runs on real hardware. If the host is asleep or offline, the line does not pick up.

🤖 She will never pretend to be human

She is warm, but she says what she is if you ask. She is an AI, and she is not a substitute for a doctor, a therapist, or a crisis line.
